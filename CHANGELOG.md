# Changelog

## 0.5.0 — 2026-10-10

- Add a corner language and region menu with Chinese, English, French and Japanese.
- Localize dates, weekday order, numbers, accessibility labels, tooltips and account flows.
- Support independent regional formats and automatic, 12-hour or 24-hour clocks.
- Persist display preferences locally without changing timestamps or quota calculations.


## 0.4.0 — 2026-10-10

First public source release.

- Local Codex managed sign-in, account-isolated history, and five-minute quota collection.
- Duration-aware short/weekly quota handling, observed activity blocks, idle gaps, reset markers, and bounded curve smoothing.
- Daily historical evidence, month/quarter/year views, and a four-hour to five-minute scale.
- Optional in-page reset reminders and reduced-motion-aware interactions.
- Synthetic preview with a stable deployment alias and configurable deployment settings.
- MIT source, application-owned SVG/motion assets, privacy and developer documentation, automated tests, and publication checks.
