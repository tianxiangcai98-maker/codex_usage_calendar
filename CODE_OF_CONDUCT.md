# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss the work without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not welcome.

Give contributors room to ask questions and make mistakes. Use minimal, sanitized reproductions. Respect the maintainers' decisions about scope and capacity.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abuse. Security disclosures belong in the private reporting channel described in `SECURITY.md`.
